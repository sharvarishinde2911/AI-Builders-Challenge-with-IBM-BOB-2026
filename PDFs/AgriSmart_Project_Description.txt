AGRISMART

AI-Powered Satellite-Based Crop Monitoring Platform

AgriSmart is an AI-powered, satellite-based platform that helps farmers monitor their crops and detect problems early.

Farmers commonly face problems like crop diseases, pests, drought, water stress, and unpredictable weather. Often, these problems are detected only after the crop is already damaged.

AgriSmart helps solve this problem through early monitoring and alerts.

MAIN FEATURES:

1. Satellite Crop Monitoring
Monitors field conditions using satellite imagery and indicators like NDVI.

2. Crop Health Detection
Shows crop status as Healthy, Attention, or Critical.

3. Water Stress and Drought Detection
Identifies possible water-related stress and drought conditions.

4. Weather Alerts
Provides crop-aware alerts based on weather conditions.

5. Crop Diagnosis
Allows farmers to upload a crop image to identify possible problems.

6. Market Information
Provides market prices and trends to support better selling decisions.

SIMPLICITY AND ACCESSIBILITY:

AgriSmart focuses on simplicity and accessibility. It provides a mobile-friendly interface with Marathi and Hindi language support.

GOAL:

The goal of AgriSmart is simple: help farmers detect problems early, understand what is happening, and take action at the right time.
